## Bounded change and evaluation

Keep model-only failed-objection audit groups artifact-only using their
producer-assigned provenance, independent of claim words, confidence or kind.
Retain their reasoning and disposition in observation artifacts; do not change
claim-graph authority, proof admission or gate outcome. Independently sourced
groups, actionable findings and material evidence gaps retain their existing
admission rules. This is a partial private-audit boundary under #1003, not a
final judgment engine or guarantee of semantic correctness.

`noise::human_output_admission_tests::ripr_8262_failed_objection_audit_stays_private_in_mixed_review`
constructs contract inputs from the public record, validates, groups,
serializes retained observations, and compiles/serializes the public review.
It is **not** a byte replay of the unavailable model packets. The unchanged
producer leaks the scoped-site reason with kind `false-premise`; the repaired
boundary must withhold it while a concrete scoped-site finding survives.

Controls include a source-supported rejection of the guarded fallback
allegation, unrelated audit prose, missing/default and alternate kinds, and
high confidence. Both true and false rejected hypotheses stay private.
`ripr_8262_public_boundary_preserves_other_evidence_sources` checks independent
proof provenance, mixed proof/audit provenance, open findings and material
gaps remain eligible. Removing the added provenance boundary must restore the
compiled regression failure; restoring it must pass.

## Filtering-order counterexample and repair

The [independent review of c2f1327](https://github.com/EffortlessMetrics/ub-review/pull/1497#pullrequestreview-5555202775)
found that filtering after claim deduplication was too late. A private audit
could win the complete claim-rank comparison and displace an independently
sourced finding, material gap or proof-backed refutation. Independently,
summary matching still saw private groups and could suppress a matching real
summary, leaving an empty public body. The preceding isolated-source controls
did not exercise these collisions and do not establish their safety.

The public renderer now removes model-only failed-objection groups after raw grouping and
before claim deduplication. This same eligible collection feeds summary
matching. Raw observations and their serialized audit remain unchanged.
This is a narrow renderer correction; the adjacent #1473 proof/decision and
stale-receipt logic retains its owner and behavior. Filtering every artifact-only
group early failed the existing cross-lane conflict suppression test in the
full baseline: those receipts carry summary-reconciliation authority despite
having no public prose. The final repair shares the existing model-only
provenance predicate and preserves other groups' previous behavior.

Two new checked-in tests reconstruct the posted counterexamples rather than
assuming access to the reviewer's separate filesystem:

- `ripr_8262_private_audit_cannot_suppress_matching_summary` compares the
  compiled public review with/without a matching validated failed objection.
  It checks nonempty finding delivery, exact public-body equality and raw
  audit retention.
- `ripr_8262_private_audit_cannot_win_public_claim_selection` checks distinct
  raw dedupe keys, compatible paths/kinds and a private group that actually
  wins the old complete rank comparison. Independent bug, missing-evidence
  and proof-refutation rows each run in both input orders. Each public body
  must equal its no-audit control, with raw audit/independent records retained.

Both tests compile and fail on c2f1327's producer at the intended lost-public-
evidence assertions. The early-filter repair must pass both. Moving filtering
back after claim deduplication must fail the independent-group test; exposing
the raw collection only to summary matching must fail the summary test. These
are output-selection controls, not source-semantic proofs. The old green
hosted gate and earlier source review do not clear this demonstrated defect;
new-head independent review remains required. Required shadow evidence stays
unproven. No protection or denied run endpoint was retried.
